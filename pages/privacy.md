### 1. General

The protection of your personal data is important to us. We process your data exclusively in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

When you visit this website, information about your access (date, time, page accessed) may be stored on the server. Simply browsing the website does not store any personal data (e.g. name, address or email address). If you use the contact form, the data you enter is processed as described in section 2.

Where personal data is collected, this is done, as far as possible, only with your prior consent. Apart from the processing described in this Privacy Policy, your data will not be passed on to third parties without your express consent.

Please note that data transmission over the internet (e.g. by email) may be subject to security gaps, and complete protection of data against access by third parties cannot be guaranteed. The provider accepts no liability for damage caused by such security gaps.

The use of the contact details published on this website by third parties for commercial purposes is expressly prohibited, unless the provider has given prior written consent. The provider reserves the right to take legal action in the event of unsolicited advertising, e.g. spam emails.

### 2. Contact Form

If you send us enquiries via our contact form, your data will be processed, stored and forwarded to us by the service Formpost.eu for the purpose of handling your request (Art. 6 (1) GDPR). Transmission to Formpost.eu takes place via the HTTPS protocol.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest). The data is stored at Formpost.eu and by us until you request deletion.

Further information: [https://formpost.eu/datenschutz](https://formpost.eu/datenschutz)
